Curbside

Practical tools and guides for integrated care teams

Like a curbside consult: quick, practical, and meant to be used back in your clinic. Here I publish tools I build with AI agents for behavioral health work in primary care, along with guides for clinicians who want to build their own.

Learn

Guides to getting started with AI agents and getting better at using them, written for clinicians, supervisors, and program leaders. No coding background assumed.

Workshop · CFHA 2026 Build the tool your setting needs Turn a recurring clinic or training problem into a six-part build brief that an AI agent can work from and that you can test.

Ground rules

  • No patient informationNothing here uses or asks for patient data. Tools run in your browser, and nothing you type is sent to a server.
  • Not clinical adviceThese are implementation and training aids. Review anything before using it in care, and follow your organization's policies.
  • A personal projectCurbside is my own work, hosted on my personal site. It is not an official University of Virginia or UVA Health resource.
